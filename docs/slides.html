Real-Time Social Listening for Patient Experience & Safety Signals
A worldwide AI-driven pharmacovigilance platform that turns unstructured patient chatter — in any country or language — into explainable, statistically-validated, regulator-ready safety signals in real time.
Worldwide · working full-stack prototype · runs offline · zero API keys required

The problem

Patients describe adverse drug reactions on social media and forums months before they surface in formal pharmacovigilance systems. That early signal is unstructured, noisy, multilingual, and full of PII.

Objective

Continuously monitor patient-generated data, extract clinical insights, and detect early safety signals — with accuracy, explainability, and compliance.

The gap

Keyword dashboards over-flag (sarcasm, negation, off-topic) and under-explain. Regulators need why, statistical weight, causality, and traceability.

  • ▸ Ingest real-time data · extract drugs/symptoms/conditions · identify AEs & sentiment
  • ▸ Detect emerging signals via analytics · explain every insight with source traceability

Solution & architecture

Worldwide extraction + explainable AE detection

Transformer biomedical NER recognizes drugs/symptoms/conditions in any region's text (lexicon fallback offline); drugs normalize to generic/ATC (RxNorm), symptoms to MedDRA PT/SOC, and medical devices to GMDN/FDA product-code with IMDRF failure modes. Then a transparent 4-gate detector (product + event) shows exactly which gates passed or failed — no black box.

Gate 1

Drug entity present (transformer + lexicon → generic/ATC)

Gate 2

Symptom entity present (→ MedDRA PT/SOC)

Gate 3

Negative sentiment (VADER + clinical boost)

Gate 4

Non-negated symptom (rule-based context)

Example: “Started Accutane and the depression hit within days. Stopped it, improved. Took it again, it came back.”

→ AE flagged (94%) · WHO-UMC Certain (temporal + dechallenge + rechallenge) · PII scrubbed · source linked.

From posts to validated signals

Disproportionality

PRR · ROR (with 95% CIs) · Yates χ², plus Bayesian shrinkage EBGM/MGPS & BCPNN IC025 — robust at small N. SDR flag on regulator thresholds.

Trend & spike

Daily buckets, EWMA-smoothed baseline, z-score spike flags — emerging signal detection that ignores weekend sparsity.

WHO-UMC causality

Deterministic Certain→Unlikely from temporal/dechallenge/rechallenge cues + risk priors (biologics, psych, Class-III).

openFDA evidence

Real US FAERS (drugs) & MAUDE (devices) report counts corroborate and boost confidence. No key needed.
